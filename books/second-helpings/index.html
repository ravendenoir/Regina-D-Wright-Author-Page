Second Helpings

by Regina D. Wright · The Marigold Brothers, Book 4

Sweet, closed-door romance

He cooks his grandmother's food so he never has to make his own. She arranges everyone's flowers and keeps none for herself. Two people who carry grief walk into the same small town.

Anatole Calogeras is the youngest Calogeras, the family's easy smile, and the one nobody can reach. Ten years ago the grandmother who raised him at her stove died, and he came home and never cooked his own food again, only hers, in someone else's kitchen, like penance. Everyone thinks he's healing. He's just gotten better at hiding the door.

Selene Okonkwo moved two hundred miles to open a flower shop where nobody knew to be careful with her. She lost her sister and business partner in the same afternoon, and now she translates every griever in town into stems and hands them back wrapped, while her own shop stays bare.
