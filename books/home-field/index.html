Home Field

by Regina D. Wright · The Tolliver Swifts, Book 3

Sweet, closed-door romance

She's spent her whole life guarding one patch of ground so nobody can take it. He's a Lambert, and taking ground is the only thing his family was ever good at.

Clem Mosely has tended the field at Solomon Field for twenty years, and she keeps it the way she keeps everything: alone, perfect, and fenced. Her people worked land they were never allowed to own, and got moved off it, generation after generation, and Clem learned the lesson in her bones. The only ground you keep is the ground you hold with both hands. She hands nobody her tools. She bungees the gate herself every morning. And she has spent her whole life making sure nothing can ever be taken from her again.
