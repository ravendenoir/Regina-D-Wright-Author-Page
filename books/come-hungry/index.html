Come Hungry

by Regina D. Wright · The Marigold Brothers, Book 3

Sweet, closed-door romance

He's the fastest man in Georgia at leaving. She's the most rooted woman in town. They've got twelve weeks.

Dimos Calogeras can charm a whole county and has never once stayed for the thank-you. Traveling physical therapist, favorite uncle, always packed, always gone before anyone gets close enough to need him. It's a good life, right up until his mother's knee surgery hands him a twelve-week contract in the one town he can't drive away from.

Zora Bell has done every head in Marigold for nine years. She runs the only Black-owned salon in three counties, keeps the whole town's secrets, and holds a standing offer from an Atlanta studio she's never let herself say yes to, because everybody here needs her, and being needed is the only kind of love she trusts.
