The Long Game

by Regina D. Wright · The Tolliver Swifts, Book 1

Sweet, closed-door romance

She's never asked anybody for anything. He's never stayed anywhere past September.

In Tolliver, Alabama, the ballpark came before the town. Dinah Tolliver's great-great-granddaddy bought the ground seven years out of bondage, her grandfather built Solomon Field on it and hung the lights, and now the Tolliver Swifts are hers: a fifth-place club, a father recovering up on the hill, and a debt coming due that could take all of it. Nobody knows. Nobody's going to know. A Tolliver holds.
