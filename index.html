About the Author

Welcome to my corner of the world.

I'm Regina D. Wright, and I write stories about the messy, beautiful, deeply human work of loving each other — through kitchens that smell like Sunday dinner, bleachers on a Friday night, and front porches where the truth always comes out eventually.

My books are rooted in community, family bonds, and the kind of slow-burn love that sneaks up on you between the second helpings and the extra innings. If you believe in found family, second chances, and the redemptive power of a really good meal — you're in the right place.

Pull up a chair. Stay a while.
