Caught Looking

by Regina D. Wright · The Tolliver Swifts, Book 2

Sweet, closed-door romance

She's spent eleven years on the radio in her father's voice. He's the first person to hear hers.

In Tolliver, Alabama, Marva Sloane is the voice of the Swifts. Eleven years in the booth, four counties listening, and every word of it belongs to her late father. She took the microphone the week they buried him and hasn't missed a broadcast since, and the whole town loves her for how much she sounds like Lou. Nobody has ever heard what Marva sounds like when she isn't being him. Least of all Marva.
