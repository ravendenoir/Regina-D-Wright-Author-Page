Made From Scratch

by Regina D. Wright · The Marigold Brothers, Book 2

Sweet, closed-door romance

Evander Calogeras is the man who feeds Marigold, Georgia. The famous olive loaf, the free day-old shelf, the funeral bread nobody's ever been charged for. Everybody loves the baker. Nobody knows he's been quietly bleeding his own savings dry to keep his mama's hundred-year-old bakery alive, because asking for help is the one recipe he never learned.

Then he misses a loan covenant, and the bank sends Octavia Reese.
