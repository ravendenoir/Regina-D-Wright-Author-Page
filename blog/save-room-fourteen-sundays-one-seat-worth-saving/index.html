Save Room: Fourteen Sundays, One Seat Worth Saving

He hasn't stepped inside his kitchen in three years.

She only came to cook Sunday dinner. ❤️

Novalee Rhone is a chef trying to rebuild her life after losing her restaurant.

Theron Calogeras is a widower who has spent three years avoiding the room that holds some of his most painful memories.

Then Novalee walks into his family's life with a menu, a little determination, and absolutely no idea how much she's about to change.

Fourteen Sundays. One Seat Worth Saving.

Save Room by Regina D. Wright is a sweet, closed-door interracial romance about second chances, family, healing, and finding love when you least expect it.

If you love grown Black heroines, small-town romance, and men who learn that opening their hearts takes more courage than keeping them closed, this one's for you.
