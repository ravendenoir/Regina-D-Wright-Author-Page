Safe at Home

by Regina D. Wright · The Tolliver Swifts, Book 4

Sweet, closed-door romance

She takes every picture in this town. She's in none of them.

For six seasons, Lena Ruiz has been the eye of Tolliver, Alabama. Twelve hundred photographs on the ballpark wall, every face this town loves, every moment that ever mattered. And if you walked that wall end to end, you would not find one single picture of the woman who took them all.

That is not an accident. It is the most deliberate thing she has ever made.

Then a sports photographer drives into town to shoot the Swifts' pennant run, and Sawyer Vance does the one thing nobody in six years has managed. He raises his camera at her.
