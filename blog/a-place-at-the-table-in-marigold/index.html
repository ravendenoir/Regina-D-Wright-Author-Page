A Place at the Table in Marigold

I've been spending quite a bit of time in Marigold, Georgia, lately.

It's a town where Sunday dinners matter, people remember who sits in which chair, and a good loaf of bread can begin a conversation that neither person expected to have.

The Calogeras family has given me several stories to share this autumn, and I wanted to tell you a little about the newest books and the one coming next.

It started with Save Room.

Save Room, the first Marigold Brothers novella, was released October 2.

Novalee Rhone is a talented chef whose restaurant has closed, leaving her to rebuild a life she worked hard to create. A standing Sunday dinner job brings her to Marigold and into the Calogeras family's kitchen.

There she meets Theron Calogeras, a quiet man who understands fixing things with his hands but has his own reasons for keeping parts of his life closed off.

I loved writing about the Sunday dinners, the family conversations, and the gradual ways two people can begin to make room for each other.

**Read Save Room**

Then came Made From Scratch.

Released November 6, Made From Scratch takes us to Evander Calogeras's bakery.

Octavia Reese arrives with a folio, a professional assignment, and a very clear understanding of what the bank expects. Evander has spent years building a business around the food he loves making, and his financial troubles are not going to disappear because the bread smells wonderful.

Naturally, the first thing he wants her to try is his olive loaf.

Their story unfolds across thirteen Tuesdays, as Octavia examines the bakery's problems and Evander begins to understand that accepting someone's expertise does not mean giving up his own.

I wanted their romance to have the warmth of the bakery without pretending that love automatically solves everything. Octavia and Evander both have work to do, and I enjoyed watching them learn to respect what the other brings to the table.

**Read Made From Scratch**

Next, we're visiting Crown & Glory.

Come Hungry, the third Marigold Brothers novella, releases December 4 and is available for preorder now.

This time, the story belongs to Zora Bell and Dimos Calogeras.

Zora owns Crown & Glory, a salon that has been part of her community for years. She knows her clients, remembers their stories, and understands how much trust can be built in an ordinary Saturday appointment.

Dimos is a traveling physical therapist who returns to Marigold to help his mother recover from surgery. His life has been built around moving from one place to the next.

Zora gives him a standing Saturday appointment. Nine o'clock, for twelve weeks.

That simple arrangement becomes the beginning of a story about belonging, independence, and discovering what it means to make room for another person without setting aside your own dreams.

**Preorder Come Hungry**

Thank you for visiting Marigold with me.

These are sweet, closed-door romances about adults who have responsibilities, histories, and lives that matter just as much as their love stories.

I hope you'll enjoy meeting Novalee and Theron, spending time with Octavia and Evander, and getting to know Zora and Dimos when their story arrives in December.

Thank you for making room on your reading list for this little Georgia town.

Warmly,

Regina D. Wright
